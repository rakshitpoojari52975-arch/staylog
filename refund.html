Refund and Cancellation Policy

StayLog · Version 2026-11-21 · Last updated 21 November 2026

Free trial

Every new account gets a 7-day free trial. No payment details are needed for it, and nothing is charged when it ends.

Subscriptions

  • Paid plans are billed in advance, monthly or yearly, through Razorpay (UPI Autopay, cards or e-mandate).
  • A plan renews automatically at the end of each period until you cancel it.
  • Cancel any time from Account settings → Cancel plan. Your plan stays active until the end of the period already paid for, and is not renewed after that.
  • If a renewal payment does not go through, your account keeps working for 3 more days while Razorpay tries again. After that it becomes read-only until the payment is made.
  • To move to another plan, cancel the current one and subscribe to the new one. The new plan starts the moment it is paid for; the days left on the old one are not refunded, so the best time is near the end of the period — or write to us and we will help.
  • After a plan ends, your account becomes read-only. Nothing is deleted for 180 days, and you can export everything.
  • Each payment has an invoice (a receipt while GST is not charged) in Account settings.

Refunds

  • Payments for a period already started are not refunded in part.
  • We refund in full: a charge made in error, a duplicate charge, or a renewal you cancelled before its date but were still charged for — if you write to us within 7 days of the charge.
  • Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay, usually within 5–7 working days of approval. [Draft note: confirm Razorpay's current refund timelines.]
